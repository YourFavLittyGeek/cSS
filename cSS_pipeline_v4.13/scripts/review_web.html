How to use the reviewer

The reading. Each candidate is a dark structure the detector found near the cortex. Scroll through it in the three planes, compare with the other sequences, then give one decision. The decision is saved at once; the next unread candidate opens (Auto-next, G). When all are read, press Q for the summary and the 0–4 score.

Y cSScurvilinear dark lining of the cortical surface / sulcus
V Veintube that continues beyond the candidate, branches, or runs into the white matter or a sinus
N Normal cortexnaturally dark cortex or sulcus, nothing abnormal
A Artifactskull, air–bone interface, motion, mask edge
U Unsurecannot decide; counted as not cSS in the score and listed for re-reading
H Near ICHsiderosis connected to a lobar haemorrhage; reported but not scored

The AI meter

The needle shows a number from 0 to 1: red below 0.33 (the detector suspects a vein, artifact or normal cortex), yellow 0.33–0.66 (uncertain), green 0.66 and above (suspects cSS). The line under it says what it suspects and why.
It is not a probability and has not been validated. It is the detector's rule-based evidence (shape, position on the cortex, both banks, vessel continuation), lowered when its vein / artifact rules fire. Use it as a second opinion only: the decision is yours. Seeing it before you decide can bias you — hide it with K for any reading that is used to test the detector.

Tools

Darkness probe gives the mean signal of the 3 × 3 voxels under the click, as a percentage of the normal cortex and in standard deviations below it (the detector's own measure: candidates start at about −2.5 SD).
Missed cSS: click on a cSS that has no outline. The point is stored with your name; add a short note in the list. Delete a point with the × next to it.
